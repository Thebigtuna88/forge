## Lens: frontend

Use the screenshots at 390px and 1440px wide (in the evidence) and the code. Check:
- Layout: overflow, clipping, horizontal scroll, overlapping or cut-off text, at both widths.
- Contrast and accessibility: text and control contrast, focus visible, labels on controls,
  images with alt text, headings in order, keyboard navigation through every control.
- States: empty, loading and error states exist and make sense; nothing assumes data is there.
- Design tokens: colours, spacing and type come from the project's tokens, not one-off values.
- No secret or private data rendered or sent to the browser.
